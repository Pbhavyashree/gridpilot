Does it work?

A year of real German grid data, October 2025 to October 2026, replayed day by day. Every forecast used only what was known at 10:00 the day before. Three everyday jobs ran once a day: a data refresh, a model retraining and a backup.

StrategyCost savedCO₂ saved
Nightly cron job at midnight0%0%
Best fixed start time, picked with hindsight46.9%40.7%
GridPilot50.2%42.0%
Perfect knowledge of the future51.9%43.7%

GridPilot reached 97% of the cost saving and 96% of the CO₂ saving that perfect foresight could have achieved.

Built by Bhavyashree Prakash. Data from Energy-Charts (Fraunhofer ISE), aWATTar and Open-Meteo. Carbon intensity is estimated from the generation mix.
